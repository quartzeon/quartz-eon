About Quartz Eon

Quartz Eon is a marketplace in Sri Lanka for digital products: VPN plans, premium accounts, e-books and more. Independent sellers run their own stores here, and you buy straight from them.

How buying works

  • Pick a product. Every product shows its seller, price and stock. Some products have options, each with its own price.
  • Pay the seller directly. At checkout you see the seller's own bank account. Transfer the exact amount and upload a photo of your slip.
  • The seller confirms. The seller checks your payment and approves the order, usually within a few hours while they are online.
  • Get your product. Your login, code, download or VPN link appears under My orders.

Why it is safe

  • Every seller store is reviewed before it goes live.
  • Stores with the Verified badge pay a monthly store fee, so they are known to us and active.
  • Each store shows whether the seller is online or away, and how to contact them.
  • If something goes wrong, use Report a problem on the order. Both the seller and Quartz Eon are told at once.
  • Quartz Eon never holds your money. It goes straight to the seller's bank account.

Sell on Quartz Eon

Have digital products to sell? Open your own store: choose Log in at the top of the shop, pick Seller and create a seller account. After your store is approved you can add products, get order alerts on Telegram and, for VPN plans, connect your own panel so customers get their link automatically.
